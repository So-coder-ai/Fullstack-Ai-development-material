# Fullstack AI Development

A practical workspace for building, revisiting, and experimenting with modern full-stack, backend, cloud, DevOps, and AI technologies.

I have previously worked primarily with a **Python-based stack**, including FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, REST APIs, and AI/LLM integrations. This repository brings together my practice and implementations as I expand that foundation into a broader production-oriented stack.

The focus is on **hands-on practice, implementation, experimentation, and revisiting concepts through code** rather than keeping isolated notes or tutorials.

---

## 🗓️ Practice Cycle

This stack is being covered through a **structured 6-month practice cycle**, progressing from frontend and backend development through databases, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, DevOps, and AI engineering.

The objective is to work through the technologies systematically while continuously applying them through implementations and projects.

```text id="0x8v2m"
Month 1
Frontend & JavaScript
        ↓
Month 2
React & Modern Frontend
        ↓
Month 3
Java & Spring Boot
        ↓
Month 4
Databases, Redis & Backend Systems
        ↓
Month 5
Microservices, Messaging, Cloud & DevOps
        ↓
Month 6
AI Engineering, RAG, Agents & Multimodal AI
```

The six-month cycle is focused on **practice and implementation**, with the repository serving as a central reference for the work produced along the way.
